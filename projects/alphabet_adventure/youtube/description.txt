Learn the first six English alphabet letters—A, B, C, D, E and F—in a colorful animated adventure for preschool kids! 🌈

Meet a shiny apple, a colorful ball, a curious cat, a friendly dog, a little elephant and a swimming fish. Look, listen, say the words, and try three fun picture quizzes!

🍎 A is for Apple
🔵 B is for Ball
🐱 C is for Cat
🐶 D is for Dog
🐘 E is for Elephant
🐟 F is for Fish

In this short lesson, children can practice:
• Recognizing uppercase and lowercase letters A–F
• Saying letter names and familiar English words
• Matching letters to pictures
• Pointing to an answer in a simple alphabet quiz

For preschool learners around ages 3–5. Parents and teachers: watch together and pause whenever your little learner needs more time to answer.
